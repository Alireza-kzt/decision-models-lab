# Private research and third-party rights

This project is private-first. No public open-source license has been selected for original lab work. The owner should choose any future release license deliberately.

Third-party models, code, datasets, papers and outputs retain their own terms. A code license does not automatically cover weights or training data. Source links and short original notes are included; third-party PDFs, weights and datasets are not redistributed in this initial package.

Before reuse or release, record the exact license file/revision and permitted uses. This document is a workflow policy, not legal advice or a grant of rights to third-party material.
